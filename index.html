[/> ABOUT ME

I’m from Standerton, Mpumalanga — a small town where “Hello, World!” felt galaxies away. Little did I know, I was called to Computer Science. In fact, CS chose me — and boy, am I glad it did and I graduated with a BSc in Computer Science and Mathematical Sciences from the University of Limpopo.

Since then, I’ve worn more hats than a `switch` statement — Lab Assistant, Software Associate Engineer, even Fashion Designer and Financial Advisor (talk about style and cents!). Whether I’m designing clean code or clean seams, I’m all about detail.

These days, I’m decoding the past while supporting the future — diving deep into legacy systems with COBOL and mainframe support at ABSA. I aspire to speak fluent JCL, keep jobs running smoothly, and if there’s a bug in the system? Consider it squashed — debugging is my superpower.

Through every version update of my life, one thing stays constant: I’m a creative coder who loves blending logic with imagination. Whether it's fixing a broken program or piecing together a new project, I always commit — with no rollback.
